About

Why this exists

I'm taking over my family's 40 rental properties across Nottingham and north Nottinghamshire: flats in the city through letting agents, and houses in Retford, Tuxford, East Markham and Allerton. My mum ran it for years from memory and a phone. That worked for her. It won't work for me, and it won't survive the 2026 rules.

So I'm building the systems from scratch and filming it: compliance trackers, Renters' Rights Act paperwork, Making Tax Digital, checking up on letting agents, and what it costs when things slip.

No courses. No "financial freedom" pitch. Just how a small UK landlord keeps the paperwork straight and the tenants safe.
